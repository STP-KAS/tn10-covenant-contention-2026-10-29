Testnet-10 only. One desk node, rusty-kaspa 2.1.0, utxoindex on, default ram-scale. The node process was already running and was not restarted. All four senders used that node's Borsh socket. Public wRPC was not used. The mini PC was not in this run.

This is the gate for the Thursday 29 Oct phase. It is not that phase. The Thursday clock in `PLAN.md` is unchanged.

## What ran

A new covenant family, path `launch::contention-2026-10-29`. Mint amount 1, limit 1, starting remaining 100000. Minter output 10 KAS, copied forward. Fee rate 200 sompi per gram on every sender.

An earlier genesis the same hour was included, then left idle. Its token output was 0.05 KAS. The builder measured storage mass 803963. The block storage limit is 500000. The token script has plurality 2, so `C * p^2 / amount` with `C = 10^12` is dominated by that output. The measured run raised the token output to 0.2 KAS. Storage mass then fit, and every later hop used that value.

Genesis for the measured run was included at 2026-10-20T18:03:17.543Z, 1301 ms after submit. The rate clock started at that second and stopped on END at 2026-10-20T18:33:33.338Z, including the 15 s grace. Steps were 5, 10, then 20 attempts per second per sender, 10 minutes each. Four senders. Same fee. Attempts in a second were shuffled. The follower advanced only when the virtual chain accepted a hop.

Full minutes landed on the attempt target: about 1200, 2400, and 4800 submits. A few minutes are 20 to 80 submits short where the wall-clock second rolled over mid-batch.

Winners stayed near 40 to 46 per full minute at every step. The 20/s minutes reached 51 once. Include p50 stayed near 1.0 to 1.1 s. The 18:05 minute was the exception, at 2007 ms. Include p95 stayed near 2.0 s in every minute.

Raising the attempt rate raised already-spent and orphan rejects. It did not raise the hop rate. One follower that waits for virtual-chain inclusion moves about as fast as that inclusion time. On this desk that was about 0.7 hops per second, while Testnet-10 produces 10 blocks per second.

Orphans were 13% of step-5 submits, 16% of step-10 submits, and 17% of step-20 submits. The rest of the non-winners were already-spent. No minute recorded a fee, mass, mempool-full, route, rpc, or other reject.
